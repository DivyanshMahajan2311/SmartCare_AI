SmartCare AI for Teens

SmartCare AI is a friendly, research-informed digital companion created to help teenagers build healthy habits, understand bodily changes, manage emotions and academic stress, and access age-appropriate guidance — all in a safe and private environment.

Mission & Vision

Mission: Empower teens with accurate, stigma-free, and practical health guidance so they can make informed choices and seek help when needed.

Vision: A world where adolescents have trustworthy digital tools to support their physical and emotional wellbeing.

Key Features

Symptom Checker
Pick symptoms to get ranked likely conditions, practical advice, and recommended specialists. Educational only — not a diagnosis.
Personal Health Tracker
Log height, weight, BMI, cycle length, mood and sleep. Save entries, view trends and export CSV summaries for care providers.
Interactive Chatbot
FAQ-driven guidance with optional external API integration for more natural conversations when enabled.
Mental Health Tools
PHQ-9 screening, peer-pressure assessment, and sentiment analysis to spot early signals and suggest next steps.
Real-time Emotion Detection (Optional)
Camera-based emotion awareness (updates at safe intervals). This feature is optional and clearly labeled for transparency.

How SmartCare Works

  1. Sign up or use Guest mode: Create a private account or use Guest mode to avoid persistent identifiers.
  2. Track quickly: Log symptoms, cycles, mood and sleep in a few taps.
  3. Analyze: Use the Symptom Checker for ranked possibilities and guided advice.
  4. Share safely: Export a clear CSV summary to show to a clinician if desired.
  5. Repeat & improve: Regular tracking increases the usefulness of trends and recommendations.

Benefits for Teens & Parents

SmartCare encourages self-awareness and timely action. It offers destigmatized information, basic mental-health screening, and practical next steps — supporting teens and giving parents controlled visibility through a dedicated Parent mode that limits access to sensitive areas.

Privacy & Safety

  • Local-first storage: Data is stored locally in SQLite by default. Users may export or delete their data.
  • Guest mode: Use without creating persistent personal identifiers.
  • Non-diagnostic intent: The app is educational — always encourage users to consult health professionals for diagnosis.
  • Parental controls: Supports restricted Parent mode so teens retain private access to sensitive modules.
  • Production advice: For production, use HTTPS, a managed DB, encryption, and robust authentication (OAuth, session protection).

Tech Stack & Architecture

Frontend: Flask + Jinja templates, responsive CSS (prototype uses inline styles).
Optional Rapid UI: Streamlit used for prototyping — can be embedded for convenience.
Backend: Flask routes, SQLite for local use, joblib/scikit-learn for ML models.
Vision & ML: DeepFace for face-based emotion detection, scikit-learn for classical models, optional LLM APIs for chatbot enhancements.
Deployment: Docker + nginx + gunicorn recommended for production; migrate to Postgres/MySQL for concurrency and backups.

Get Involved & FAQ

Q: Is SmartCare a medical service?

A: No — SmartCare provides educational guidance and tools. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Q: Where is my data stored?

A: By default data is stored locally (SQLite). You may export CSVs and delete your data. For production, store encrypted on a secure server.

Q: Can parents see everything?

A: No. Parent mode provides limited access. Teens retain full access to most modules to protect privacy.
